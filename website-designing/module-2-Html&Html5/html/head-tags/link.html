Paragard is a hormone-free copper intrauterine device (IUD) that provides over 99% effective, long-acting birth control for up to 10-12 years. It prevents pregnancy by continuously releasing natural copper ions, which act as a spermicide, and can also be used as emergency contraception if inserted within 5 days of unprotected sex.
